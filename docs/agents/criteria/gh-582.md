| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| AC-3 | test | WHEN the `sandbox-probe` row fails on a user-namespace error and neither the nested state nor the AppArmor restriction applies THE SYSTEM SHALL give generic user-namespace advice with no nested-sandbox or AppArmor fix |  |
