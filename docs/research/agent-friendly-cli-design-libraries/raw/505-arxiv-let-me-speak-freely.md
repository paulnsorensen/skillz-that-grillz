Title: Let Me Speak Freely? A Study on the Impact of Format Restrictions on Performance of Large Language Models (Tam, Wu, Tsai, Lin, Lee, Chen; Aug 2024)

> We observe a significant decline in LLMs reasoning abilities under format restrictions. Furthermore, we find that stricter format constraints generally lead to greater performance degradation in reasoning tasks.

Not TOON-specific (JSON/XML constrained generation vs free-form), but directly relevant to point 2/4: forcing any rigid output schema (including JSON mode) can cost reasoning accuracy, independent of TOON.
